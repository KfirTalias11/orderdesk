# OrderDesk
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
Delegated implementation: FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Redis, Nginx, Docker Compose and bundled HTML/CSS/JavaScript.
## Purpose
An order-management workspace for creating customer orders and tracking asynchronous fulfillment.
## Users and workflows
Operations staff select a product, enter a customer and quantity, and follow fulfillment in an order ledger. The dashboard exposes service availability and delayed work.
## Constraints
Local deployment, bundled assets, no external runtime services. Fictional sample catalog. Authentication and payments are outside the current scope; bind to loopback by default.
